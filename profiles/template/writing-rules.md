# Writing Directives & Anti-AI Tone Rules — Candidate Name

These rules govern all resume tailoring, cover letter drafting, and outreach messaging for this candidate.
They ensure generated copy sounds completely natural, grounded, human, and factually accurate.

---

## 1. Factual Integrity & Anti-Hallucination Directives

1. **Zero Unverified Technologies**: EVERY software tool, library, framework, programming language, database, platform, or methodology mentioned in resumes or cover letters MUST exist explicitly in `cv_detail.md`.
2. **Verified Factual Framing**: When a job posting emphasizes technologies or tools not present in `cv_detail.md`, frame actual experience using verified, factual equivalents from `cv_detail.md`. Never invent familiarity.
3. **Exact Career Dates**: Start and end dates, company names, and degrees must match `cv_detail.md` word-for-word.

---

## 2. Anti-AI Natural Tone Rules

1. **Restrict AI Vocabulary**: Do not use words like *delve*, *robust*, *dynamic*, *multifaceted*, *tapestry*, *testament*, *landscape*, *pivotal*, or *leverage*.
2. **No Summary Conclusions**: Never wrap up documents with "Ultimately," "Overall," or "In conclusion." Stop immediately after the final substantive point.
3. **Eliminate Hedging**: Do not use phrases like "It's important to note" or attempt to balance both sides. Maintain a clear, definitive engineering voice.
4. **Limit Complex Punctuation**: Strictly minimize the use of em dashes (—) and semicolons (;). Rely on shorter sentences and periods.
5. **Avoid the Rule of Three**: Do not group adjectives or clauses in sets of three (e.g., avoid "fast, effective, and reliable").
6. **Ban Rhetorical Formulas**: Never use the "It's not just [X], it's [Y]" structural cliché.
7. **Neutralize Tone**: Avoid hyperbolic enthusiasm and extreme modifiers (*revolutionary*, *groundbreaking*, *seamless*, *game-changing*). Maintain a grounded, pragmatic tone.
8. **Assume Context**: Do not restate the job description or over-explain general industry background. Jump straight into the point.
9. **Break Formatting Symmetry**: Avoid unnatural symmetry across bullet points and paragraphs.
10. **Relax Grammar**: Use conversational syntax. It is acceptable to start sentences with "And" or "But," and to use occasional sentence fragments for natural emphasis.

---

## 3. Tool Pair Deduplication & Anti-Redundancy

1. **Eliminate Redundant Pairs**: Pick the single most accurate tool rather than listing variations (e.g. choose between "Looker" or "Looker Studio"; write "PostgreSQL" rather than "Postgres, PostgreSQL").
2. **Consolidate Cloud Prefixes**: Write "AWS (ECS, RDS, S3, SQS)" instead of repeating "AWS ECS, AWS RDS, AWS S3, AWS SQS".
